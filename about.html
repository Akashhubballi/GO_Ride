Our Journey

Empowering Seamless Urban Movement

GoRide was created to eliminate city traffic friction through transparent technology, verified drivers, and accessible multi-modal transport.

Company Background

Pioneering Safe & Dependable Rides for Modern India

Founded with the conviction that getting from point A to point B should never involve unexpected price surges, driver cancellations, or safety worries, GoRide connects commuters with thousands of verified independent driver-partners.

By integrating bike taxis for traffic dodging, auto rickshaws for quick doorstep accessibility, and premium sedans and SUVs for family comfort, GoRide serves every transit need within one unified interface.

Our Core Principles

  • 01.
    Predictable Upfront Fares: No surge spikes when you step out into the rain. Fares are locked prior to trip confirmation.
  • 02.
    Uncompromised Rider & Driver Safety: In-app SOS, background-checked partners, and live telemetry tracking.
  • 03.
    Sustainability by Design: Actively transitioning 40% of our fleet to electric vehicles by 2027.
