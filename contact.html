Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our booking process, fares, and safety policies.

How are GoRide fares calculated?
GoRide uses transparent, upfront pricing. Our system calculates fares based on estimated journey distance, typical traffic conditions, and vehicle category. You see your exact price prior to confirming, with no surge surprises at the end of the trip.
What is the 4-digit Ride PIN for?
Every GoRide booking generates a unique 4-digit security PIN. You must verbally share this PIN with your driver upon boarding. The driver's application will not start the meter or trip without this verification, guaranteeing you are in the correct authorized vehicle.
Can I cancel my ride booking?
Yes, rides can be cancelled free of charge within 2 minutes of driver assignment. After 2 minutes, a nominal cancellation fee applies to fairly compensate the driver partner who has already navigated toward your pickup location.
What safety measures are in place during late-night rides?
All rides are monitored in real time by our Safety Command Center. Riders can utilize our 1-touch SOS emergency button, share live GPS trips with family, and benefit from 100% background-verified driver partners.
